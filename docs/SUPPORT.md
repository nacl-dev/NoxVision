## Hardware & Community Support

NoxVision is free and open source.

Because NoxVision communicates directly with physical thermal cameras, continued development and compatibility testing require access to the actual hardware. Thermal cameras are expensive, and as an independent developer it is not practical to purchase every supported model personally.

Community support can help make additional development hardware possible.

### How hardware priorities are decided

There is currently **no fixed hardware funding goal**.

Future development hardware will be selected based on:

* Community requests
* The number of users affected
* Models that are not yet sufficiently tested
* Features that require specific hardware for development or verification

If you own a Guide camera that is not sufficiently supported by NoxVision, please let us know through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/nacl-dev/NoxVision/issues).

Community feedback helps determine which devices should be prioritized for future testing.

### What your support helps fund

Community support may be used for:

* Thermal cameras for compatibility testing
* Hardware required to support additional Guide models
* Android test devices and accessories
* Development and testing infrastructure
* Continued maintenance and new features
